\caption{The three corpora, in their own words. Each middle-column item is a verbatim test
sentence with its gold label; the right column shows what structure the model is given.
\textsc{mc-real} and \textsc{rp} come with a pregroup parse, drawn here as \texttt{lambeq}
builds it, and the curved wires are the grammar cups that make a sentence's meaning a
contraction rather than a bag of tokens. The scale contrast is the point of this figure:
\textsc{mc-real} and \textsc{rp} resolve accuracy only to one part in thirty, so a single test
sentence moves the score by more than three points, whereas \textsc{sst2} is the one corpus
here at a size where a difference of a few points means anything. This is why the paper draws
its load-bearing conclusions on \textsc{sst2}.}
