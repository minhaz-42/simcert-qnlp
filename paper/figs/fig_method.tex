\caption{The audit protocol. A sentence the corpus actually contains enters as the
compositional parse \texttt{lambeq} builds for it; the curved wires are the grammar cups that
make its meaning a contraction rather than a bag of tokens. Whichever of the five trained
models consumes it, the harness sees only the parameter-bound circuit that model emits, which
is what makes the audit model-agnostic. That circuit is re-simulated as a matrix product state
whose bond dimension $\chi$ is swept upward from a product state, and at each $\chi$ the
truncated device is asked for the same decision. The smallest $\chi$ whose predictions match
the untruncated model within tolerance is $\chi^\star$, Eq.~\ref{eq:chistar}. A model whose
$\chi^\star$ stays small and flat in system size has no quantum resource left to appeal to.}
\label{fig:method}
