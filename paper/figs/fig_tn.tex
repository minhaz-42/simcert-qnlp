\caption{\textbf{The audit in tensor-network terms.} Any trained variational model is a matrix
product state (MPS) whose bond dimension caps the entanglement it carries \citep{shin2024}. For
each bond we take the singular-value spectrum (centre) and keep only the largest $\chi$ values,
discarding the rest, which is the standard MPS truncation. \textsc{SimCert} contracts the
resulting $\chi$-capped MPS (right; teal tensors, orange physical legs $q_i$, red readout
observables) and asks how small $\chi$ can be before the recovered prediction $\hat y^{(\chi)}$
changes. For every audited model the answer is $\chi{=}1$, a product state, or $\chi{=}2$, whereas
an exact representation would need bond dimension $2^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}$.}
\label{fig:tn}
