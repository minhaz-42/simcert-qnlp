\caption{\textbf{The audit in tensor-network terms.} Any trained variational model is a
matrix product state (MPS) whose bond dimension caps the entanglement it
carries~\citep{shin2024}. \textsc{SimCert} truncates that MPS to bond dimension $\chi$ (right;
blue bond legs, orange physical legs $q_i$, red readout observables) and asks how small $\chi$
can be before the recovered prediction $\hat y^{(\chi)}$ changes. For every audited model the
answer is $\chi{=}1$ (a product state) or $\chi{=}2$, while an \emph{exact} representation would
need bond dimension $2^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}$.}
