\caption{What is audited, and the class the audit certifies. Top, the four datasets, each
with one test-set sentence quoted verbatim; \textsc{sst-2} rows are clipped phrases and
arrive pre-tokenised, which is why its example reads as a fragment and carries spaced
punctuation. The $\chi^\star$ column lists the values actually observed on that dataset
across every model and seed. \textsc{mc} and \textsc{rp} show both $1$ and $2$ because the
compositional model needs $\chi{=}2$ there while the attention and mixer models need
$\chi{=}1$. Bottom, the nested hierarchy: a classically simulable model (CSIM) is a special
case of one simulable up to a data-loading step (QESIM), which is a special case of
quantum-simulable (QSIM) \citep{cerezo2025,shin2024}. \textsc{SimCert} certifies membership
in CSIM whenever the trained state truncates to a small bond dimension, and we make no
claim outside that innermost class.}
